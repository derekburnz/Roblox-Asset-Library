## Icon (all three)
1. The net covers the cat's face. At 50 px the hero reads as a net mesh, not a cat. Put the hoop just above the head, about to drop (the hoop frames the ears), so both eyes are clear.
2. The top third is empty (a big sparkle on flat burst). Scale the cat up about 25% and move it up so its ears or hat touch the 8% margin. The eyes should land at about 40% height. Trending tiles have no empty sky.
3. The bacon is small and half off the left edge. Give him a bust at about 40 to 45% of the height, bottom left, face fully inside the 8% margin, with the big grin (set 2 close-up).
4. Word: keep it in the bottom 25%, but inside the 8% side margins (the rounded crop eats the C and the !). Use T.fit(word, 0.84 * W, style). CATCH! reads best at 50 px (6 letters). SECRET! and MYTHIC! hold at 100 px but blur at 50.
5. Colour on the dark Home page: orange (V2) stands out most. Blue (V1) sits too close to the blue trending tiles (Tsunami, Throw Food). Violet (V3) is fine. Suggestion: the orange burst with the Secret cat, or the orange burst with the Mythic, and A/B against blue.
6. One sparkle at most, and put it on the cat, not in the sky.
