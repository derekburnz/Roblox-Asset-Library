Intro cutscene storyboard

A 24.3 second fly-through that plays before a new player can move. It opens with your first flight in the Cat Cannon: you land at the Alleyway's camp, catch your first cat, carry it into the camp and give it a job back in your cafe. Then it tours the other camps and the town, one short caption per shot, and ends with you at the cannon. A small Skip button sits bottom right with a bar that fills for 5 seconds before it can be pressed, and a Tutorial button at the top replays the intro until the player dismisses it.

Shot by shot

The order follows what a new player needs most: how to get to the cats, what to do with one, why it pays, where it leads, then the extras. Nobody can skip before 5 seconds, so everyone sees the cannon, the flight and the camp. Captions stay generic about controls ("tap") so one line works on phone, PC and console; the real prompts teach the keys.

On screen during the intro

The cutscene frame

  • Letterbox. Thin black bars top and bottom say "cutscene". The HUD, prompts and world markers are hidden and the character is frozen at the spawn.
  • Caption. One line, bottom centre, in the game's FredokaOne with the dark outline, at most about 45 characters so a phone shows it on one line. It pops in on every cut.
  • Shot dots. Ten small dots on the top bar show how short it is, which keeps more people watching.
  • The flight. In shot 2 the real flight's clouds fill the screen for a moment with the name card "To the Alleyway!", then part over the camp, the same look the player gets on every flight.
  • Skip. A small outlined "Skip »" pill on the bottom bar, right side, on screen from the start but dimmed, with a gold bar filling inside it for 5 seconds (Derek, 28 Oct). When it is full the pill brightens with a small pop; then tapping it, or gamepad B, ends the intro on the spot. A tap before that only shakes it. On a replay it is full at once. At least 44 px tall on phones.
  • Ending. After shot 10 or on Skip, the camera returns to the character at the spawn, the bars slide away, the HUD fades in and the first-flight hint points at the cannon.
