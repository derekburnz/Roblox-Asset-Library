# Roblox tycoon, restaurant and simulator onboarding and progression gates (first 60 seconds, first 3 minutes, rank up, rebirth, areas)

Research date: 2026-11-20. Read this first about source quality:

- Strongest sources: Roblox's own Creator Hub docs and its DevForum funnel post (primary, with a few real numbers), the GameDiscoverCo piece on Grow a Garden (developer quotes, May 2025), and requirement tables from guide sites for Steal a Brainrot, Pet Simulator 99 and Bee Swarm Simulator.
- Weak sources: the per-game "beginner guide" articles (Sportskeeda, Deltia's, Pro Game Guides and similar) describe the order of first steps but almost never give seconds or prices. RoWatcher and ROLearn articles give confident numbers with no dataset behind them; they are marked "unsourced" wherever used.
- I found no public tutorial completion rate, first session length or D1 figure for any of the named games. That is a real gap, not an oversight.
- The fetch budget ran out before I could open pages for Grow a Garden's Ascension, Theme Park Tycoon 2's tutorial, My Restaurant's floors, and any Roblox cat cafe game. Those are listed under Gaps, with my unverified recollection labelled as such.
- DevForum redirected one thread to a maintenance page during the session (the "Learn More About First-Time User Experience & Onboarding" announcement), so that thread is not covered.

## 1. For each game: what does the player do first and how soon? Is the first customer or first income automatic? Is there a tutorial, and is it forced, skippable or absent?

### Takeaway
The big restaurant and tycoon games put the player into a tiny, already working business within the first few steps (a chair and table and $350 in Restaurant Tycoon 2, a free plot in Restaurant Tycoon 3, a free base in Steal a Brainrot, a free garden plot in Grow a Garden), and customers or income arrive on their own once one object is placed or bought. Tutorials, where they exist, are short and shown in the world (highlights, arrows, trails) or are optional; the two largest 2025 hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot) are described by guides without any tutorial sequence at all.

### Cited Findings

Restaurant Tycoon 2 (Ultraw, 2018)
- The player starts with $350, one Comfy Chair and one Small Dining Table; the guide's first advice is where to place them (near the kitchen door). — [narcosis-the-game.com RT2 beginners guide, dated July 2026](https://www.narcosis-the-game.com/?p=2002)
- First income is automatic after seating exists: customers sit, order, eat and leave cash, but the player must walk over the cash to pick it up, roughly every 30 to 45 seconds because uncollected cash despawns. — [narcosis-the-game.com](https://www.narcosis-the-game.com/?p=2002)
- The game "walks you through basic cooking controls"; cooking is a timed button press minigame where imperfect food still sells and the perfect bonus is small (so a new player cannot fail the first dish). — [narcosis-the-game.com](https://www.narcosis-the-game.com/?p=2002)
- Pace: one in-game day is about 8 real minutes and a first day should earn roughly $100 to $300, against a $350 starting balance. — [narcosis-the-game.com](https://www.narcosis-the-game.com/?p=2002)
- Staff come later: the guide's rule is to hire the first employee at 4 or more tables. Unlocks are tied to a 1 to 5 star rating rather than a level number. — [narcosis-the-game.com](https://www.narcosis-the-game.com/?p=2002)
- A second guide (for the same game under the name "My Restaurant 2") repeats the $350 start and 8 minute day, says the player may start from scratch or from a pre-built template, tells players not to skip "tutorial hints", and gives dish times of 10 seconds for Mexican dishes versus 30 seconds or more for other cuisines. — [beatcopgame.com, dated August 2026](https://beatcopgame.com/my-restaurant-2-in-roblox-beginner-guide/)

Restaurant Tycoon 3
- First steps in order: pick a free plot anywhere on the map, pick a restaurant style (trendy, modern, wooden, lounge), pick a cuisine (Indian, English, French and others), enter as owner, and "the game assigns you guests to serve"; then seat guests, take orders, pick a starter dish and cook it with actions such as mixing, kneading, frying and chopping. — [Sportskeeda RT3 beginner's guide](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/restaurant-tycoon-3-a-beginner-s-guide)
- Progression after that: levels unlock dishes (starters, mains, desserts), missions and quests also unlock dishes, skills (Chopping, Baking, Frying, Serving) shorten cooking, there are 16 staff in total (8 waiters, 8 chefs), and plot growth is an "Expansion" tool under Upgrades paid with cash. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/restaurant-tycoon-3-a-beginner-s-guide)

My Restaurant! (BIG Games, released 2019-12-06; older game)
- Player starts with a basic simple space, hires waiters and cooks, and unlocks new floors to add capacity; there is no maximum level. The page gives no floor prices or requirements. — [allroblox.com My Restaurant page](https://allroblox.com/en/game/my-restaurant)

My Hello Kitty Cafe (Rock Panda Games, created 2022-04-12)
- "A short tutorial comes first, after which you're free to build your cafe." Employees take orders, deliver food and clean tables; the player earns XP faster and more money by doing the tasks personally. — [Attack of the Fanboy, last updated 2022-11-02 (old)](https://attackofthefanboy.com/guides/roblox-my-hello-kitty-cafe-tips-tricks-how-to-level-up-fast-and-earn-coins/)
- Roblox's own onboarding docs use this game as the model for a guided tutorial that does not use text: it spotlights the one button to press (dimming the rest of the screen, with an outline or arrow), "clearer than telling players to click a backpack button", and it draws directional trails on the ground that "suggest a path without forcing it". — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques)
- Scale: 522.9M visits, peak 20.5K concurrent, about 200 concurrent in late 2026. A paid teardown lists a "20 minute average playtime". — [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/experience/3494556606); [Metaverse Marcom teardown listing](https://www.metaversemarcom.io/product-page/my-hello-kitty-cafe-game-teardown-case-study)

Retail Tycoon 2 (Widgeon)
- The tutorial is optional ("can opt to complete a tutorial"). Opening a store takes several build steps before any income: claim an empty plot, build walls, floor and roof, place at least one checkout counter, add shelving, buy stock; a cashier is needed at the start and the player delivers goods personally (instant delivery costs 25% of goods value). — [Deltia's Gaming RT2 beginner's guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=249723)

Lumber Tycoon 2 (Defaultio; older game)
- No real tutorial: the game "drops you into Lumberland with $20, no axe, and almost no instructions". The Basic Hatchet costs $12 (leaving $8), one good oak sold whole "may earn more than $100", a starter plot costs $100, and selling that plot's wooden SOLD sign pays a random $400 to $600. Next goals are priced as a ladder: Shabby Sawmill $220, Small Truck $400, Fair Sawmill $800, Plain Axe $90, Steel Axe $190, Hardened Axe $550. — [allthings.how LT2 first axe to first sawmill](https://allthings.how/roblox-lumber-tycoon-2-how-to-play-from-first-axe-to-first-sawmill/)

Theme Park Tycoon 2 (Den_S)
- Guides recommend the Junior Coaster as the cheapest first ride and a way to learn the editor; guests need connected paths, entrances and exits or they get stuck and unhappy. Achievements raise the park rating. — [Pro Game Guides TPT2 beginner's guide](https://progameguides.com/roblox/roblox-theme-park-tycoon-2-beginners-guide-theme-park-tycoon-2-tips-and-tricks/)

Work at a Pizza Place (Dued1; older game)
- Six jobs are open at once (Manager, Cashier, Cook, Pizza Boxer, Delivery Driver, Supplier); the player gets a free Shack house; no pay accrues while inside the house. The guide describes no tutorial. House prices form the long ladder: Tiny House 100 coins, Small 1,200, Medium 3,700, Large 5,200, up to Estate 600,000. — [Sportskeeda definitive guide](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/work-pizza-place-a-definitive-guide)

Welcome to Bloxburg (cafe and job play)
- New players spawn in a public area; job tutorials are contextual: "Tutorials appear for first-time workers" when a job is started. Pizza delivery is the recommended first job, Bloxy Burgers is the food job; repeated shifts raise pay. — [welcome-to-bloxburg.pages.dev beginner tutorial (fan wiki)](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.pages.dev/guides/beginner-tutorial/)

Steal a Brainrot (2025)
- First steps: spawn, claim a base, buy one brainrot from the moving line with starting cash (for example Noobini Pizzanini); it then produces a steady income. A new base holds up to 8 brainrots. Playing with friends gives a 10% income boost. No tutorial is described. — [Deltia's Gaming, How to play Steal a Brainrot](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=259152)
- Another guide gives the opening as "claim a base and buy the first three Common brainrots", whose combined income should reach the next price tier in about 8 minutes. — [rblxguide.com SaB beginner guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/steal-a-brainrot/beginner-guide)

Grow a Garden (2025)
- The player receives a garden plot on joining, buys the cheapest seeds from the Seed Shop with a small starting amount of Sheckles (Carrot or Strawberry are the suggested first seeds), plants, waits, harvests and sells. Guides mention no in-game tutorial. — [gag.gg beginners guide, updated 2026-06-22](https://gag.gg/guides/beginners-guide); [ROLearn Grow a Garden guide](https://rolearn.dev/guides/grow-a-garden/)
- Analyst view: Roblox "has little patience for tutorials" and the game's simple interface suits players "from 6 to 99". — [GameDiscoverCo newsletter, 2025-05-27](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)

Pet Simulator 99 and Bee Swarm Simulator (openings)
- PS99: Spawn (Area 1) is unlocked by default and the first purchases are area gates priced in coins. — [bloxodes PS99 areas](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/areas)
- Bee Swarm: five fields are open to every player at the start (Blue Flower, Clover, Dandelion, Mushroom, Sunflower). — [bloxodes Bee Swarm fields](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/bee-swarm-simulator/fields)

Roblox's own worked examples (useful because they have numbers)
- In the docs' example game "Plant", most playtesters pressed the first seed button within 10 seconds, so the highlight on it is delayed to 11 seconds and shown only to players who have not acted. — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques)
- In the same game's first funnel, step 1 (in farm) was 100% and step 2 (plant a seed) was 29.52%, a 70.48% loss at the very first action, and fewer than 3% reached the last of 9 steps. The fix was an icon over the pots showing where a seed can go. — [DevForum, Improving Onboarding through Funnel Events (Roblox staff post)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improving-onboarding-through-funnel-events/3064458)

### Inferences
- Pattern across the restaurant games: the first income needs exactly one working seat or object, and the customer comes to the player. The player's first "job" is collecting or serving, not building. Retail Tycoon 2 is the outlier (walls, floor, roof, counter, shelf and stock before any sale), and it is also the one whose tutorial is described as optional, which suggests a heavier start needs more teaching.
- Restaurant Tycoon 2's numbers imply the starting balance ($350) is worth roughly one to three in-game days (8 to 24 minutes) of early income. So the first purchase decisions are made with gift money, not earned money (inference from the guide's figures).
- Lumber Tycoon 2's opening is a hidden gift: land costs $100 but selling the sign on it returns $400 to $600. The first "purchase" pays the player 4 to 6 times its price. That is a cheap way to make the first minute feel generous without a tutorial.
- The Roblox "Plant" data is the clearest warning in this set: a 70% loss happened between standing in the farm and doing the first action, and the cure was a world icon, not text.

### Gaps
- No source gave seconds to first customer or first coin for any restaurant game. I could not time them; a hands-on playtest of RT2, RT3 and My Hello Kitty Cafe with a stopwatch is the only way to get this.
- Whether RT2, RT3 and My Hello Kitty Cafe tutorials can be skipped is not stated in any source I could open.
- Theme Park Tycoon 2: starting money, whether its tutorial is forced, and how rides unlock were not in the guide I read. Unverified recollection: it offers a guided tutorial at first join that can be declined, and ride unlocks come from achievements and park rating.
- Cat cafe or pet cafe games on Roblox: a search returned only bare roblox.com game links with no descriptions, and the budget ran out before I could open them. I found no guide coverage of any Roblox cat cafe game, which itself suggests none is large. The non-Roblox "Cat Cafe Manager" (Steam, Switch) showed up instead and was excluded.
- Cook Burgers: nothing found. Unverified recollection: it has no tutorial and no progression, players simply walk in and cook.
- My Restaurant (BIG Games): floor unlock requirements not found.
- Steal a Brainrot: starting cash, first brainrot price and income per second were not in any guide I could open.

## 2. How do classic "button" tycoons teach with no text at all, and why does that pattern work for young players?

### Takeaway
The classic tycoon is a single chain: one button starts income, the player saves, steps on the next button, and repeats; the world itself (moving conveyor, rising money, one visible next purchase) is the instruction. Roblox's own guidance explains why this suits young players: visuals need no reading or translation and survive small phone screens. No source offered measured data on the pattern, and developers on the DevForum also call it repetitive once the chain is the whole game.

### Cited Findings
- DevForum description of the loop: a player "starts at a single button that begins earning currency, saves up, steps on the next button, and repeats indefinitely"; most tycoons use "a dropper system". — [DevForum, Different approach to tycoons, 2020-04-20 (old thread)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/different-approach-to-tycoons/533253)
- Same thread, the criticism: "Linear tycoons themselves are repetitive" (colbert2677) and "Repetition discourages replay-ability" (amp_lex); posters point to open-ended goals as the fix, naming Lumber Tycoon 2's base building, Bloxburg's house building, Miner's Haven, Theme Park Tycoon 2 and Restaurant Tycoon 2 as the non-linear tycoons that lasted. — [DevForum, 2020](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/different-approach-to-tycoons/533253)
- Claim that a new player understands a dropper tycoon in about 30 seconds with no tutorial because "the moving conveyor, rising money, and visible upgrade button communicate the loop"; the tycoon is described as four parts (dropper, conveyor, collector, upgrade) forming a closed loop with no dead ends, and progress is a physical build the player can see. Unsourced opinion, no data. — [RoWatcher, Tycoon games: the genre that built Roblox](https://rowatcher.com/news/tycoon-games-the-genre-that-built-roblox-and-still-hasn-t-peaked)
- Roblox on why visual teaching works: visuals "need no translation, help young or pre-reading players, stay readable on small mobile screens"; cues should sit in the world in the player's line of sight and stay on screen until the step is done; Jailbreak uses footprints to hint a route "without explicit instructions". — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques)
- Roblox on contextual teaching: tutorials should be "triggered by natural play" (entering a zone, picking something up) instead of a fixed sequence, which gives "faster access to fun, and lower cognitive load in the first session". The Squishmallows game shows its combine tutorial only once the player holds two identical items, and its marketplace tutorial only when the player finds the marketplace. — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques)
- Timed hints: show help only after a delay, only once, and only to players who have not acted; Roblox suggests A/B testing a 5 second versus 10 second delay. — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques)
- An unsourced design rule from the same family of articles: feedback should follow a player action within about half a second, and a game should teach one mechanic at a time. — [RoWatcher, Onboarding without tutorials (opinion, no data)](https://rowatcher.com/news/onboarding-without-tutorials-teaching-through-play-in-roblox)

### Inferences
- Why the button chain works for children, pulling the cited pieces together (inference): (a) there is exactly one affordable thing at a time, so there is no choice to get wrong; (b) the price floats over the object, so the goal and the cost are the same visual; (c) the next button appears only after the last is bought, which is a contextual tutorial in Roblox's sense, triggered by play and not by a script of steps; (d) nothing has to be read.
- The pattern's weakness is the same as its strength: a single chain has no decisions. The games that held players for years kept the "one obvious next purchase" for the first minutes and then opened into free building. A cafe start can borrow the first part without the second.
- "Feels pushy" in a step tutorial maps onto what Roblox's docs warn about: fixed sequences and cues that block play. The docs' alternative is hints that appear late and only for the stuck player.

### Gaps
- No measured data on button tycoons versus tutorial tycoons (completion, D1) was found. Everything on "why it works" is design reasoning.
- Mega Mansion Tycoon specifically: no source opened. Unverified recollection: it is a standard button chain with a rebirth after the last button.
- No RDC talk on tycoon onboarding was found in search results.

## 3. How do these games gate the next tier, rebirth or area: a single currency price, or a collection or checklist requirement? Any developer commentary or data on which keeps more players?

### Takeaway
All three models are live in top games: pure currency (Pet Simulator 99's early areas at 900, 2.5k, 8k, 20k coins), pure collection count (Bee Swarm Simulator's gates at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 35 bees), and currency plus named collection items (Steal a Brainrot's rebirths, $500K plus two specific brainrots for the first). The consistent pattern is that the first gates in a session are currency only or trivially low counts, and specific-item requirements arrive later; I found no developer statement or data comparing retention between the two.

### Cited Findings

Currency only
- Pet Simulator 99 early areas: Spawn free, Colorful Forest 900 coins, Castle 2.5k, Green Forest 8k, Autumn 20k. None of the first five lists a rank, rebirth or task requirement. Later areas change currency (gold bars, platinum) and some add task gates that "may ask you to hatch eggs, make gold or rainbow pets, or break special objects first". — [bloxodes PS99 areas](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/areas)
- Lumber Tycoon 2: every early gate is a price tag (axe $12, land $100, sawmill $220, truck $400). — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/roblox-lumber-tycoon-2-how-to-play-from-first-axe-to-first-sawmill/)
- Work at a Pizza Place: house tiers are coin prices only, from free to 600,000. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/work-pizza-place-a-definitive-guide)
- Restaurant Tycoon 3: "Cash unlocks most of the game, from staff to building"; dishes unlock by level and by missions. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/restaurant-tycoon-3-a-beginner-s-guide)

Collection count
- Bee Swarm Simulator: zone gates require owning a number of bees, not paying currency. Fields by bees required: 5 (Bamboo, Spider, Strawberry), 10 (Blue Brick, Mixed Brick, Pineapple, Red Brick, Stump, White Brick), 15 (Cactus, Pine Tree, Pumpkin, Rose), 20 (Ant, Hub), 25 (Mountain Top), 35 (Coconut, Pepper). — [bloxodes Bee Swarm fields](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/bee-swarm-simulator/fields)
- The first gate (5 bees) is deliberately soft: other gates teleport the player back to spawn if they lack the bees, but the Basic Bee Gate does not. — [Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki, Basic Bee Gate](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate)

Currency plus specific items
- Steal a Brainrot rebirths need cash and named brainrots: Rebirth 1 $500K plus Trippi Troppi and Gangster Footera; 2 $1.5M plus two named; 3 $12.5M plus two; 4 $35M plus two; 5 $100M plus two; from Rebirth 6 ($350M) one named brainrot each, up to Rebirth 19 at $30Qa. Each rebirth gives a cash multiplier equal to its number, a starting cash bonus ($5K at Rebirth 1), +10 seconds of base lock, a slot (levels 2 to 19) and new shop gear. — [Beebom, Steal a Brainrot rebirth guide](https://beebom.com/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide-levels-and-rewards/)
- Conflict: one guide says to plan the first rebirth "around $50M in total cash banked" and the second at $250M, which does not match the $500K and $1.5M requirement table. It may be strategy advice for a later game version or simply wrong. — [rblxguide.com](https://rblxguide.com/games/steal-a-brainrot/beginner-guide); contradicted by [Beebom](https://beebom.com/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide-levels-and-rewards/)

Checklist or rank goals
- Pet Simulator 99 has 40 ranks (Noob to Herald). Each rank draws from a pool of "possible goals" (6 at Rank 1, 53 at Ranks 21 to 40) with a count of rewards (9 at Rank 1, 30 at high ranks); rank raises pet slots (7 at Rank 1 to 10 at Rank 2) and egg capacity; "some ranks list a required zone or rebirth". — [bloxodes PS99 ranks](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/ranks)
- Pet Simulator 99 rebirths are tied to place, not collection: shrines sit at areas 25, 50, 75, 99, 125, 150, 175, 199 and 219. — [holdtoreset.com PS99 rebirth system](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-rebirth-system-explained/)
- Conflict on PS99 rebirth cost and reset: one source lists diamond costs (10,000 for Rebirth 1, 25,000, 50,000, 100,000, 500,000 for Rebirth 6) and says modern rebirths "don't reset progression"; another lists no fee and says the player loses coins and area progress while keeping pets and unlocks. The game changed its rebirth rules over time, so the no-reset version is probably the current one, but I could not confirm. — [bloxodes PS99 rebirths](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/rebirths); contradicted by [holdtoreset.com](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-rebirth-system-explained/)

Developer commentary and data
- Roblox ties D7 (not D1) to the progression system: "clear short- and long-term goals, content variety, and difficulty balanced so users neither run out of content nor get frustrated". — [Roblox Creator Hub, Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- Roblox recommends low early thresholds: use "low early thresholds so players level up quickly" and tune them live. — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding)

### Inferences
- Reading the three ladders side by side (inference): the first gate in every one of these games is reachable in the first session without knowing anything. PS99's first area is 900 coins. Bee Swarm's first gate is 5 bees and forgives failure. Steal a Brainrot's collection requirement does not appear until the first rebirth, well after the first session's opening minutes.
- Collection gates carry a specific risk that currency gates do not: the player can have the money and still be blocked by luck or by not knowing where the named item comes from. Steal a Brainrot softens this by dropping from two named items to one from Rebirth 6 onward, and by showing the requirement on a button that is always on screen.
- A count gate ("own 5 bees") is gentler than a named-item gate ("own Trippi Troppi") because any item counts. If a collection requirement is kept anywhere, a count is the low-friction form.
- PS99 separates the two ideas cleanly: movement through the world is paid in coins, while the checklist (rank goals) pays out rewards and capacity instead of blocking the road. That is a way to keep a collection or quest system without gatekeeping areas behind it.
- Step ratios: PS99 early areas rise about 2.5 to 3 times per step (900, 2.5k, 8k, 20k). Steal a Brainrot rebirth cash rises 3 times, then about 8 times, then roughly 3 times (500K, 1.5M, 12.5M, 35M, 100M, 350M, 1B).

### Gaps
- No developer commentary, A/B result or analyst data comparing currency-only gates with collection gates for retention was found. This question remains unanswered by public sources.
- Grow a Garden's Ascension (its rebirth, added in 2025) was found only as search result titles: guides exist on "Ascend", "Garden Coins" and a "Garden Coin Shop" ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/grow-a-garden-ascend-guide-how-to-rebirth), [gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/all-about-ascension-in-grow-a-garden-gag-garden-coins-orsons-new/z38515)). I could not open them. Unverified recollection: Ascension asks for a Sheckle amount plus one specific requested plant and pays Garden Coins, which would make it a currency plus named-item gate like Steal a Brainrot.
- Theme Park Tycoon 2 and Retail Tycoon 2 land expansion costs and My Restaurant floor requirements were not found.

## 4. How is rank up or rebirth made quick and satisfying (one button, a big celebration, the new area shown at once)?

### Takeaway
The documented pattern is a permanently visible rebirth button that doubles as the requirements list, a single confirm press, and a reward bundle that changes play at once (multiplier, starting cash, a new slot, new gear). Roblox's own guidance asks for a deliberate "moment of joy" at the end of onboarding; I found no source describing the celebration effects of any specific game.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot: "Click the Rebirth button on the left side of the screen" to see what is needed, collect the cash and brainrots, then click the same button again to rebirth. The reset wipes brainrots and cash; what comes back immediately is a cash multiplier, a starting cash bonus, an extra slot, longer base lock and newly unlocked shop gear. — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide-levels-and-rewards/)
- Pet Simulator 99: the player reaches a Golden Cat Rebirth Shrine in the world, interacts, sees a menu of the rewards, and presses one green "Rebirth!" button. Rewards are concrete feature unlocks, not only numbers: Rebirth 1 gives auto hatch, a teleport cannon, a key and +75% pet strength; Rebirth 2 clans; Rebirth 3 pet teams; Rebirth 4 a rocket to a second world. — [holdtoreset.com](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-rebirth-system-explained/); [bloxodes PS99 rebirths](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/rebirths)
- Roblox guidance: "Close onboarding with a deliberate milestone reward, animation, or celebratory effect so players want to return", and keep short, mid and long-term goals visible in the world or UI; Jailbreak highlights season rewards in a bright colour. — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding)
- Roblox guidance: deliver "a rewarding moment after users first complete the core loop". — [Roblox Creator Hub, Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- Prestige as status: the rebirth count becomes a visible social marker that gives finished players a reason to continue. Unsourced opinion. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/tycoon-games-the-genre-that-built-roblox-and-still-hasn-t-peaked)

### Inferences
- The two games solve "where do I rank up" differently: Steal a Brainrot keeps the button on the HUD from the first second, so the goal is always known; PS99 puts a shrine in the path, so the player walks into it. Both avoid a menu the player has to discover.
- The requirement list and the action are the same control in Steal a Brainrot. The player never needs a separate checklist screen. For a coin-only rank up this collapses further: one button showing one price, lit when affordable.
- Rewards that add a capability (a slot, auto hatch, a new tool) are what the guides list first, ahead of the multiplier. This suggests the capability is what players read as the prize (inference from how guides present them).
- A first rank up that resets nothing is the trend: PS99 moved to rebirths that keep progress, by one source's account. A reset on the first rank up is the riskier choice for young players.

### Gaps
- No source describes the visual or sound celebration at rebirth in any of these games, or whether the new area is shown at once. This needs direct play or video review.
- No data on how soon the first rebirth or rank up lands in a first session.

## 5. How do they handle idle or offline income and the reason to come back tomorrow, in the first session?

### Takeaway
Grow a Garden is the clear case: "Grows Offline" is printed on the game's icon, and its operator says this brings players back to see what grew; weekly Saturday updates with a gift add a calendar reason. The classic restaurant games do the opposite inside a session, making the player collect cash by hand or lose it. I found no description of offline earnings in the restaurant games.

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden's icon says "Grows Offline"; the article calls it unusual for Roblox and says it brings players back to see what they grew, and lets them play other games and return. — [GameDiscoverCo, 2025-05-27](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- "Your plants keep growing even while you are offline." — [gag.gg](https://gag.gg/guides/beginners-guide). Detail: one guide says mutations do not happen offline, while shop stock and event timers keep running. — [ROLearn](https://rolearn.dev/guides/grow-a-garden/)
- Grow a Garden ships updates every Saturday at 7am PST, and each week more players reportedly arrive for the update gift; operator Janzen Madsen calls the weekly drop under-used on Roblox. The game peaked at 8.9M concurrent and ran 1.5M to 2.5M outside spikes at the time. — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- Restaurant Tycoon 2: cash is left on tables and must be collected by walking over it about every 30 to 45 seconds or it despawns; tip jar income is manual unless the auto collect game pass is owned. — [narcosis-the-game.com](https://www.narcosis-the-game.com/?p=2002); [beatcopgame.com](https://beatcopgame.com/my-restaurant-2-in-roblox-beginner-guide/)
- Work at a Pizza Place: no pay accrues while the player is inside their house (an anti-idle rule). — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/work-pizza-place-a-definitive-guide)
- My Hello Kitty Cafe: daily tasks refresh and pay diamonds, gacha tickets and sometimes money. — [Attack of the Fanboy, 2022 (old)](https://attackofthefanboy.com/guides/roblox-my-hello-kitty-cafe-tips-tricks-how-to-level-up-fast-and-earn-coins/)
- Claim that tycoons "demand active presence, unlike idle games". Unsourced. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/tycoon-games-the-genre-that-built-roblox-and-still-hasn-t-peaked)

### Inferences
- Grow a Garden tells the player about offline progress before they even join (it is on the icon). The return reason is set up in the first session by planting something that will not be ready until later. The promise is made by the first action, not by a popup.
- Putting the offline promise in the store icon also works as marketing copy, not only as a mechanic.
- Restaurant Tycoon 2's despawning cash is an older design that punishes looking away. With "no dark patterns for a young audience" as a rule, the Grow a Garden direction (things get better while you are away) fits better than the RT2 direction (things are lost if you are slow).

### Gaps
- Whether Restaurant Tycoon 2 or 3, My Restaurant, My Hello Kitty Cafe, Retail Tycoon 2 or Steal a Brainrot pay offline earnings, and how they present them on return, was not stated in any source read. Unverified recollection: Steal a Brainrot bases keep earning while the owner is offline up to a cap, and several modern cafe tycoons show a "while you were away" payout on join.
- No source describes a first-session daily reward or streak screen in these games in enough detail to cite.

## 6. What do developers say about first session length, tutorial completion and D1 in these games?

### Takeaway
No developer of the named games has published tutorial completion, first session length or D1 figures that I could find. What exists is Roblox's platform guidance (reach the fun in 5 minutes or less, prefer brief or contextual teaching, measure each first-session step, fix the earliest biggest drop first) and unsourced third-party benchmarks that should be treated as estimates.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox: aim to get users to the fun "ideally in 5 minutes or less after entering your game"; recommends "a brief tutorial or contextual tooltips rather than long tutorials"; track completion at each first-session step and fix large drops. — [Roblox Creator Hub, Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- Roblox: onboarding success is measured by D1 retention; players "often decide whether they like a game within minutes"; test individual tutorial steps (for example shorter dialogue versus a guided arrow) and starting currency amounts with experiments. — [Roblox Creator Hub, Onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding)
- Roblox staff: start with the largest and earliest drop in the funnel, since it usually gives the biggest gain; prioritise drop-off fixes over content updates; improvements should show within a few days; if D1 is at or near benchmark for similar games, further onboarding changes matter less. The example funnels were 6 and 9 steps covering the first few minutes. — [DevForum, Improving Onboarding through Funnel Events](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improving-onboarding-through-funnel-events/3064458)
- Roblox staff: developers "often can't see their game from a new player's perspective", so drop-off causes (boredom, confusion, blockage, bugs) should be checked with fresh playtesters. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improving-onboarding-through-funnel-events/3064458)
- My Hello Kitty Cafe: "20 minute average playtime" (marketing page for a paid teardown; date and method not given). — [Metaverse Marcom](https://www.metaversemarcom.io/product-page/my-hello-kitty-cafe-game-teardown-case-study)
- Grow a Garden retention is described only as "stellar", with no percentages. — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- Unsourced benchmark tiers: D1 under 20% critical, 20 to 30% below average, 30 to 40% good, 40 to 50% excellent, 50% or more exceptional; simulator targets D1 35% (good) to 50% and D7 18% to 28%; "tycoon: first machine within 30 seconds"; each second of non-gameplay time said to cost 2 to 3% of a cohort. The page cites only unnamed "2025-2026 platform data", and one of its own worked examples has arithmetic that does not hold. Treat as estimates. — [ROLearn, First week retention optimization](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/first-week-retention-optimization)
- Unsourced opinion pieces argue that step tutorials of three to five minutes raise first session length but hurt return rates, and that the first 60 to 90 seconds should be playable; neither article has data. — [RoWatcher, onboarding paradox](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-onboarding-paradox-fewer-tutorials-better-day-3); [RoWatcher, onboarding without tutorials](https://rowatcher.com/news/onboarding-without-tutorials-teaching-through-play-in-roblox)

### Inferences
- The only hard first-session number in this whole set is Roblox's own: 70% of players lost before the first action when the first action was not visually obvious. That supports spending effort on the first 10 seconds over polishing later steps.
- Roblox's "10 seconds, then a highlight at 11" is a usable default for a hint that does not feel pushy: most players never see it.
- Since no named game publishes D1, the practical yardstick is Roblox's benchmark comparison inside Creator Hub analytics for similar experiences, plus a custom funnel of 6 to 9 first-session events.

### Gaps
- No tutorial completion rate, D1 or first session length was found for Restaurant Tycoon 2 or 3, My Restaurant, Retail Tycoon 2, Theme Park Tycoon 2, Steal a Brainrot, Pet Simulator 99 or Bee Swarm Simulator.
- No RDC talk, Naavik or Deconstructor of Fun teardown of a Roblox tycoon's first session turned up in search. Naavik has a piece titled "Predicting the next big hits on Roblox" that I did not open.
- The DevForum announcement "Learn More About First-Time User Experience & Onboarding" could not be read (maintenance redirect); its content is probably mirrored by the two Creator Hub pages cited above.
