# Voiceover lines: Cat Companion week and the Christmas teasers (Design-Agent for Growth, Nov 18 2026)

These are extra AI voiceover variants for Growth's week 1 briefs (Growth-Agent/social-2026-11-18/week-2026-11-19.md). Each clip has four takes: Story ("In this world..."), Cute, Funny and Hype. Motion-FX can cut 2 of them per clip, and Growth keeps the winning style after week 2.

**Rules for every line:**
- Every fact is true in the live game.
- No Robux, no "free", no "limited time", no countdown pressure.
- Secret cats only appear as a dark shape.
- Each take runs 6 to 12 s spoken, and the hook comes in the first 2 s.
- Every take ends on the searchable name.
- Moments versions use no voice at all (Roblox rule), so these lines are for TikTok, Reels and Shorts only.

**End line, used on every take:** "Catch a Cat Cafe. On Roblox."

---
## Fri Nov 20: Cat Companion
- **Story:** "In this world... when you rank up, one of your cats decides you're its person. It follows you everywhere. And somehow... you get richer."
- **Cute:** "Rank up once, and this little guy follows you everywhere. Through the town. Into the cannon. Everywhere. He's my best friend now."
- **Funny:** "I hired a cat. He doesn't work. He just follows me around. And my money went UP? Best employee ever."
- **Hype:** "Your best cat can follow you. More money AND more luck. The rarer the cat, the bigger the boost. Who are you picking?"
(The Hype line says Money and Luck together. That is only true once #5296 is live. Until then, cut it to "More money or more luck.")

## Sun Nov 22: Cats run the cafe
- **Story:** "In this world... cats don't just nap. They make the coffee, carry the cake and greet every guest. I just collect the money."
- **Cute:** "Tiny barista. Tiny waiter. Tiny greeter. Big cafe energy."
