# Christmas Event content pitch (Design-Agent, Nov 18 2026)

Draft for Growth's launch pack slots. Derek can change any of it; the team goes ahead on this unless he does.

## The four slots
| Slot | Pitch |
|---|---|
| Area | **Snowglobe Village**: walk into the giant snow globe in Town Square and you shrink into a tiny snowy village inside it, with a fireplace lodge, a lit tree and a gift stall. |
| Christmas cats | **8**, all kept after Jan 3 (same shape as Harvest Hollow: four Common, two Rare, one Epic, one Legendary). |
| Tier gate | **Tier 2**, same as Harvest Hollow, so new players reach it on their first day. |
| Code | **SNOWGLOBE** at launch. |

## One-line area pitch for the Nov 23 post
"Step inside a giant snow globe and find a tiny snowy village full of Christmas cats."

## Why this shape
- It reuses Harvest Hollow's plumbing (a limited area, a portal at Tier 2, kept cats that earn like a cat two rarities lower in your newest area, a stall of things to keep). It's a content job, not a systems job, so it fits before Dec 12.
- The giant snow globe is already a model (Model-Agent's GiantSnowGlobe in the #5153 banner), so the portal and the teaser picture match the banner.
- The banner cats (Hot Chocolate, Elf, Snow Boots, Flurry) are existing Snowy Peak cats. The 8 event cats are new, so the Nov 30 "8 Christmas cats" tease is true.

## Draft cat list (names original; teasers show dark shapes only, per the secret cats rule)
Gingerbread (Common), Tinsel (Common), Cocoa Mug (Common), Snowball (Common), Candy Cane (Rare), Jingle (Rare), Mistletoe (Epic), Star Topper (Legendary).
