# First 60 seconds for Home players (Design-Agent spec, 2026-11-16)

Source: Growth-Agent brief Growth-Agent/home-recs-2026-11-16/briefs/4-first-60-seconds.md and the #4307 comment.
Problem: 35.56% of Home players leave at 61 to 180 s, phone worst.

## What the live flow does today (bowl first, catchFirst false)
1. Loading screen with Skip (v752), then the intro shots (Skip from second 0).
2. Strip asks for food in the bowl; first food lands, the bait cat arrives in about 5 s (FirstBaitWaitSeconds), the FirstBaitCut cutscene plays, then the Tame card.
3. That first bait cat is already the "wow" cat: Bait.wowRarity makes it Rare (85%) or Epic (15%).
4. The first hand catch (Onboarding first_catch) comes later, in the Alleyway after the cannon. #4814/#4817, #4815/#4818, #4816 cover it.

So the "first cat" a new player gets is the bowl cat, and it is already rare. It just does not look rare: cats have no rarity glow.

## Decisions

### A. The first cat in hand inside 30 s of play on phone
- "First catch" for this goal means the bowl cat claimed (Tame pressed), not the Alleyway catch. Under bowl first that is the first cat a player owns.
- Target: Tame pressed at most 30 s after the loading screen ends, median, on phone.
- Steps, all tutorial only:
  1. The moment the intro ends, the food step's button gets the tutorial ring and arrow (TutorialGlow, with dim) on every device, not only after an idle wait.
  2. Keep FirstBaitWaitSeconds 5 and the cutscene; the cutscene already has tap to skip.
  3. The Tame card's button gets the ring as soon as the card shows.
- Measure: seconds from step_time "loaded" (or the loading screen's end) to first_bait_resolved, by device. If those two are not both logged with device, add one step_time "first_cat_owned" with the seconds since loaded and the device.

### B. A rare cat that looks rare in the first minute (the thumbnail promise)
- Use the wow bait cat. No scripted extra cat.
- From the moment it appears at the bowl until it is claimed or let go: a Highlight outline in its rarity colour (Rare blue, Epic purple, from the existing rarity palette), a slow soft sparkle around it, and a small "RARE!" or "EPIC!" tag over its head.
- The FirstBaitCut camera already frames it, so the glow is on screen in the first shot it appears in.
- On claim, the tag pops and the sparkle bursts once, then everything goes (owned cats keep no glow, to keep phones light).
- Reduce Motion: outline and tag only, no sparkles. Phone budget: one Highlight, one emitter at 10 to 15 particles.
- Owner: Motion-FX (client Effects). The server already marks the bait as wow (bait.wow); the client reads that.

### C. No pop-up before the first cat is owned
- Until the bowl cat is claimed (stats.luresUsed above 0 and no wow bait waiting), only the tutorial strip, its ring and arrow, the cutscene and the Tame card may show.
- Everything else waits until 10 s after the claim, then shows one at a time as it does today: toasts, the What's New board, Notify Me and event RSVP prompts, invite and group prompts, starter or store cards, admin event intros (they show as the small strip only), restart banners, Daily Treat.
- Owner: Gameplay-Engineer. QuietStart is the place: add a "beforeFirstCat" gate next to its tier gate, and have the AdminFlash quietWhen probe read it too.
