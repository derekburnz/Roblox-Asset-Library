# Ideas to ask Derek about (content and quality of life)

Started 2026-11-01 by the Design-Agent thread, from Derek's 2026-10-29 17:30 ask: "Start also planning out some idea suggestions for content or improvements / qol that can suggest and ask about also to improve the game". Per the launch plan, all of this waits until the 7 day ads week shows home recommendations. The first four are things Derek asked for himself between 10-28 and 10-30.

## Content, in the order I would build it

1. **Leaderboards** (Derek, 10-28 17:14). Boards in the plaza and a tab in the Index for Index completion, playtime and money per second, the top 50 for all time and for this week. They are cheap to build, and players come back to check their place.
2. **A Rainbow coat above gold** (Derek, 10-30 00:24). It rolls at about one tenth of the gold chance and earns x3 (gold is x1.5). It shimmers through every colour and leaves a short sparkle trail when it walks, so it reads as better than gold from across a street. It also gets its own Index tab.
3. **A Rescue Rally weekend** (Derek, 10-30 00:38: "some sort of group content with your friends to get rewards together", not Halloween). For two days, every rescue in a server fills one shared bar. At 25%, 50% and 100%, everyone in the server gets a prize: golden fish, a Rally Crate and, at 100%, a costume for one cat. It uses Team Rescue (F92) and rescues that are already built, and it rewards players for being online at the same time.
4. **Trading from Tier 3** (Derek, 10-30 00:23). Cats and decor only, never coins or golden fish. Both players see both offers, and any change resets both Accept buttons. There is a 5 second countdown after both accept, and a log of past trades. Locked cats cannot be offered. This one needs the most safety work, so it comes last.
5. **Cat team battles**, the parked auto battler idea (Derek, 10-30 00:38). Three of your cats against three of a friend's in a short cafe-off judged on their stats, for small prizes. A bigger build, best after the Rally shows whether group content lands.
6. **One extra thing at each area's camp** (Set 4 Q4): a Fish Pond in the Woods, a Yard Sale in the Neighbourhood and a Lost and Found in the Sewer. The cannon picker shows when each is ready ("Fish Pond: 3 fish waiting"), so a trip there is a choice, not a chore.

## Quality of life

7. **Refill the bowl.** When the bowl empties and the player has more of the same food, a "Refill" button sits on the bait card, so there is no trip through the bag.
8. **Equip best after a catch.** An optional switch that runs Equip best whenever a new cat is checked in.
9. **Sell extra Commons.** One button that sells every unlocked Common the player has a second copy of, with a confirm that lists them. One of each always stays.
10. **Lighter cafes for phones.** A setting that hides other cafes' customers and animations beyond 40 studs.
11. **A "what changed" card.** Shown once after each update with three lines and pictures, as the What's New board in town does.
12. **Pin a goal.** Tap any rank up requirement or quest to pin it to the goal card.
