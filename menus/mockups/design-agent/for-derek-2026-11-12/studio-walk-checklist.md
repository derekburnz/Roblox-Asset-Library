# Studio walk: last night's builds

For Derek, in Studio on the dev place. About 15 minutes. Each step says what to do and what you should see. Mark any step that looks wrong and keep going.

Before you start, in the dev place turn these two switches on: `cartTier6` and `secretCats`. Use a test save at Tier 6 or higher with a few billion coins.

## 1. Town Square (2 minutes)
1. Spawn. You should see the cannon in the middle and the stations spread around the edge, none crowding another.
2. Walk a full circle round the cannon. You should pass, in the corners and along the edges: Arcade, Spin Wheel, Rally board, Golden Fish Market, Day Cart, Leaderboards, Night Market cart, Feedback stand, Thank You board, Lucky cat sign, Mystery Market, Codes Cat and Bellhop.
3. Every station faces the cannon, and there is clear floor between any two of them.
4. Nothing should block the street or the walk from spawn to the cannon.

## 2. Bigger circles (2 minutes)
5. Walk onto the circle in front of the Spin Wheel. The circle is clearly bigger than before and the window opens as you step on it.
6. Step off. The window closes.
7. Do the same at the Codes Cat and the Fish Market. Only the station you are standing at opens, never two.
8. Fly to the Park. Step on each camp station's circle. One station opens at a time, and no bench, tree or prop stands inside a circle.

## 3. Rummage Bin (3 minutes)
9. Fly to the Alleyway. The bin is off to the right side of the entrance, not in the way, and you can see it from where you land.
10. A "!" floats over it when it is ready.
11. Step up and rummage. The bar needs 5 hits. The zone is small and the marker is a bit faster than a rescue.
12. Win. A card reads "You found:" with an icon, name and amount for each thing. It closes after about 4 seconds or when you tap it.
13. Check your bag: what the card showed is what you got.
14. The "!" is gone and the prompt reads "Empty for now".

## 4. Oasis Spa sort (1 minute)
15. Fly to the Desert and open the Oasis Spa.
16. The cat list has a Sort control with Rarity and Level. It opens on Rarity, best rarity first.
17. Press Level. The highest level cats come first. Press Rarity to go back.

## 5. Top player line (1 minute)
18. This one needs a top 10 leaderboard account joining, so it can't be seen alone in Studio. Skip it here. Review-QA checks it with a test.

## 6. Day Cart top shelf (3 minutes)
19. Back in the Town Square, open the Day Cart. Scroll to the bottom.
20. You should see Honey Cream (1B), Comet Treat (3B), Big Lucky Potion (5B), Big Gold Rush Potion (8B) and Mega Money Potion (20B), on the days they are in stock. Each has its own picture.
21. Buy one. Your coins drop by its price and it lands in your bag.
22. On a save below Tier 6 the same rows show locked with "Tier 6".

## 7. Secret cats (3 minutes)
23. Ask for a test Secret to be spawned in the Park (Review-QA has the command).
24. Everyone gets a banner and a chat line: "A Secret cat is somewhere in the Park!"
25. In the Park, look for a thin beam of light going up to the sky. Walk to it.
26. The cat has a dark haze and its tag reads SECRET and "???".
27. Catch it. It takes 8 circles and is harder than a Mythical.
28. After the catch, a bigger catch effect plays and the chat names the cat.
29. Open the Index. A Secret page is now at the end, showing only the one you found. On a save with no Secret, that page is not there at all.
30. In the cat room, the Secret earns far more than your best Mythical.
