## What it means
The click is the problem, not what happens after. People who do click stay 15 minutes on day 1, which is fine. But only about 1 in 200 who see the tile click it, against about 1 in 50 in the genre middle. Roblox then shows us less.

1. Let the new thumbnails reach about 1,000 impressions each (about Nov 19 to 20). Then turn off the losers and keep the best two plus BACK PACK. Read Nov 20.
2. Icon: the icon sits in front of every impression, so it matters most. The new icon went up Nov 16. Judge it with the Nov 20 read; if play through is still under 0.5%, test a bigger, closer cat face.
3. The 61 to 180 s bounce (27%) is the bowl and Rank Up stretch. rankUpRightAway (on today) and the first-60-seconds work target it, which also helps the Home signal.
