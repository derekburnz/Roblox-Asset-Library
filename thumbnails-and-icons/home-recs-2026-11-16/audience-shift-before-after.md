# Audience shift: did the game get worse or did the mix change? (Growth-Agent, 2026-11-16)
Data: analytics runs 43885994607 (days 6, Nov 10 to 14, Nov 15 not filled yet) and 43858355281 (Nov 14 only, 1,314 new players). Before = Nov 10 to 13 (female 18+ ads), after = Nov 14 (both campaigns all ages). Before numbers are days6 minus Nov 14. Session averages by device are approximate (Roblox averages are per day, not weighted).

## Answer
Derek's theory is mostly right, with one correction: it is a younger and unverified audience, plus more tablets and fewer computers, not more phones (phone share stayed 44% to 43%). Part of the drop may still be real game friction; the numbers cannot fully separate the two yet.

## Mix change (new players)
- Age: 18+ was 91% before and 24% on Nov 14. "Age unchecked" went from 0 to 888 of 1,314 (68%); ages 9 to 12 and under 9 appear (96 players, none before).
- Device: tablet 19% to 35%, computer 24% to 11%, console 13% to 10%, phone 44% to 43%.
- Gender: female stayed about 73% to 87%. Gender did not change; age did.
- Volume: 187, 839, 1,070, 1,169 then 1,314 new players a day.

## Reading
- Same device, shorter sessions: phone down about a third, tablet down about 60%. A device mix change alone does not explain that, age and play skill do. Younger and unverified players leave sooner and pay less (payer conversion 1.4% to 0.28%, as expected for under 13 players who can buy less).
- Computer got better (18 to 27 min), so the game itself did not break for strong devices.
- So: the mix changed a lot, and the per device drop is consistent with younger players. But Nov 14 also had game changes (second cat, tier ladder, new areas on Nov 15) so some of the drop could be the game. To split it cleanly we need age by session length per day (not in the Creator Hub export; AgeGroupV2 gives counts and D1 only).

## What to do

4. Ads: the all ages campaign brings volume but weak sessions. Derek decides budgets; I would not change them before the Nov 17 read.
5. Roblox's own signals only watch players from Recommended For You, so a younger Home audience will also read worse on play rate. Thumbnails with cute, clear pictures fit this audience.
