# Home recommendations: genre, what is good, what to change (Growth-Agent, 2026-11-16)

Derek asked (14:24 UTC): is the game in the right genre, is 12,000 home impressions a lot, and what to change after home recs (thumbnails, detail page, title).

## 1. Genre
- Roblox's own list (create.roblox.com experience genres) has Simulation with subgenres Idle, Incremental Simulator, Physics Sim, Sandbox, Tycoon and Vehicle Sim. Roleplay and avatar sim has Animal Sim and Pet Care.
- Best fit: Genre Simulation, subgenre Tycoon (run and upgrade a cafe, money per second). Backup: Idle. Pet Care is weaker because we catch cats, we do not care for them.
- Action for Derek or Sync: open Creator Hub > Game details (Publish settings) and check the genre and subgenre on the place. If it says anything outside Simulation, change it. A wrong genre means wrong benchmark group and wrong Home audience. I could not read the saved value from the dashboard text; the Overview benchmark lines (play through rate genre 50th 2.15%, 90th 4.33%) show we are being compared with a collect/simulation group, which is consistent.
- Our real hook (catch cats, rarity, cafe) is "collect and tycoon". Trending neighbours (Grow a Garden, Steal a Pet, Fisch) are all Simulation. Keep it there.

## 2. Is 12,000 a lot?
- Our numbers (Acquisition > Home recommendations, last 56 days): about 11,000 users saw us on Home in ONE day (Nov 15), 193 a day averaged over 56 days. 41 of them played.
- Play rate from Home: 41 / about 11,000 = 0.37% (Overall tab shows Home Recommendation conversion 0.37%).
- Roblox publishes no absolute "good" number. The benchmark is per genre and shown as a line on the chart. Our Overview play through rate is 0.06% (1st percentile) against a genre 50th of 2.15% and 90th of 4.33%. Treat 2% as okay and 4% as great, on the Overview basis.
- So: 11,000 in a day is a good SIGN (Roblox's explore phase showed us to a real cohort; many small games never get past a few hundred), but it is not a lot in absolute terms (a front-page game gets millions). And the 0.37% is about 6 times under the genre median. At 2.15% those 11,000 would have been about 235 plays, not 41.
- Not interested rate on Explore: 34.83 per million impressions (daily average). No benchmark shown; low.
- First play bounce: 61 to 180 s bucket 35.56%. Under 60 s: no data yet. Day 1 playtime from Home players: 10.9 min.
- Caution: Roblox ranks Recommended For You on players who ARRIVED from it, so the 41 are the whole signal. Impressions only keep growing if play rate, bounce and day 2 to 7 return look healthy.

## 3. What to change (ranked)
1. Thumbnails first. Home impressions come with the thumbnail, so this is the largest lever on 0.37%. Roblox's own advice: show clearly what players will find, match theme and colour to the audience, keep key text off the bottom edge (player count covers it), 16:9, 1920x1080. Keep 2 to 5 thumbnails active for personalization (Roblox reports average +8.5% play through rate, some +50%).
   - Our test (Nov 14 to 15): 4 thumbnails, 4,276 impressions, 12 qualified plays (0.28%). Back Pack 0.59%, Cat Treats 0.34%, Secret Cats 0.25%, 3 New Worlds 0.23%. Gaps are 1 to 3 plays, no call yet. Plan stays: drop the lowest on Nov 22.
   - For a general (all ages, mostly phone) audience: one big cat close up doing the verb (net catching a cat), rarity or money word big and readable at phone size, bright colours, almost no title text (Roblox prints the name under the tile). See Research-Agent/notes/thumbnail-study-2026-11-02.md. Money-only or silhouette pictures that sell 18+ "find it" curiosity are the weak ones for kids.
   - Swap a thumbnail in with each update (Roblox advice), then hold until the next update.
2. Video. Gameplay video autoplays on Recommended For You (up to +39% playtime reported). We have one approved; uploads reset Dec 9. Make sure the first 3 seconds are a catch.
3. Icon. Same rule: one cat, high contrast, readable at tiny phone size. Read CTR after the new icon (Overview > Impressions) before swapping again.
4. Detail page: first line says the verb and the hook ("Catch cats, run a cafe, ride a cannon to work"); then the update list; keep the 8 detail pictures in the order in thumbnails-2026-11-14/detail-page-8-order.png. Weak detail pages hurt Home play rate.
5. Title: keep "Catch a Cat Cafe". It says the verb (catch), the noun (cat) and the setting (cafe). Do not add keyword stuffing.
6. After the click (this is where the 41 become 235): first 60 seconds. 35.56% of Home players leave in 61 to 180 s. The loading screen, bowl-first tutorial and phone fixes are the real fix; thumbnails only decide who clicks.
