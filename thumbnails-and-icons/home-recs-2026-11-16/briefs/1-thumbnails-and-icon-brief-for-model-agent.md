# Home thumbnails and icon brief for Model-Agent (Growth-Agent, 2026-11-16)
Goal: lift Home play rate from 0.37% toward the genre median (2.15%) for an all ages, mostly phone audience. Derek wants real quality: judge each picture at 150 px wide on a phone, next to Grow a Garden, Steal a Pet and Fisch style tiles.

## What top Simulation and Tycoon tiles do (Research-Agent/notes/thumbnail-study-2026-11-02.md, 33 games)
1. One big hero doing the game's verb, 40 to 70% of the frame height, close camera.
2. A creature or payoff on the opposite side, joined by motion (arc, speed lines).
3. One number or rarity word, very big, thick dark outline, top third.
4. Almost no title text (Roblox prints the name under the tile).
5. Bright, saturated, high contrast; key things away from the bottom edge (player count covers it).

## Our weak spots (from Nov 14 to 15 test: Back Pack 0.59%, Cat Treats 0.34%, Secret Cats 0.25%, 3 New Worlds 0.23%)
- The four weaker ones sell an UPDATE (names, areas, silhouettes). A cold Home visitor does not know the game yet. Back Pack won because it shows a cute thing a kid understands in one second.
- General audience (many under 13, phone): cute, bright, clear. Dark silhouettes and money only pictures read as 18+ curiosity bait.

## Deliver (1920x1080 png, saved under /mnt/project-files/Model-Agent/home-thumbs-2026-11-16/ plus a 150 px contact sheet)
Set of 5, keep Back Pack as the control:
A. "Catch it!" the net player mid swing at a fluffy Rainbow or Mythical cat that is glowing, speed lines, big word MYTHICAL (or 1 IN 100). The core verb.
B. "Your cats work for you" cafe interior, three cats in aprons serving, big +$ number, warm light. The tycoon half.
C. Cute collection: a bright grid or parade of 6 to 8 different cats (Common to Legendary) with rarity colours. Easy to read on a phone.
D. The cannon: player and cat flying out of the cannon over the town, big and silly. Our one unusual thing.
E. Back Pack (control, existing).
Retire from rotation: Secret Cats and 3 New Worlds as Home thumbnails (keep them on the detail page as update pictures).
Rules: no cat in the bottom 15%; no more than 3 words; no title; every cat must be a real in game cat (no false promises); renders from the game, not new art.
