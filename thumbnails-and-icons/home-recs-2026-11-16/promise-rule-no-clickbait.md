# RULE: the icon, thumbnail and title must match the first minutes (Derek 2026-11-16 18:45 UTC)
Roblox's new Home ranking (June 15 2026) was built to kill clickbait: if the icon or title promise one thing and players join and get another, they bounce in the first 60 s or 61 to 180 s and that hurts rank (sources: Roblox docs and RDC in video-research-2026-11-16, files 01, 12, 15). Ad pictures are not counted in ranking but the same rule saves ad money.
## Check before any icon, thumbnail, title or detail-page line goes live
1. List what a new player really sees and does in the first 3 minutes (from the build, not from memory).
2. Every promise in the picture or words must appear in that list. If it does not, either show it early or do not promise it.
3. Lead with what they actually do: catching cats with the net, the cat cannon, the cafe. Show the REAL thing, one big subject, readable face.
## Audit of the current set against the live first minutes (Design #4920: first bait cat is a guaranteed Rare or better with a big reveal card at about 30 to 40 s; bowl then cannon then Alleyway then catch is quick; Epic share raised in #4921)
| Promise | Delivered by 60 s? | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Catch a cat with the net (Catch It, bacon + net) | Yes | Keep as lead |
| Cat cannon (D Cannon) | Yes, bowl to cannon is in the first minutes | Keep |
| Rare or Epic cat reveal | Yes, at 30 to 40 s | Show a glowing Rare or Epic as the hero cat, not Mythic |
| Mythic or Secret cat as the hero of a Home thumbnail or the icon | NO, not in the first minutes | Do not make it the hero. Allowed only as a small corner "find them later" tease, or after Design adds a visible glowing Mythic or Secret early |
| "$1,000,000" (B Cats Work) | NO money like that is seen early | Drop the number or reword to what they see (cafe plus cats), Model-Agent to rework |
| "100+ CATS!" (C Collect) | Partly (they see 1 to 3 cats) | Keep as a goal line but with real cats shown and the catch action as the main picture |
| Back Pack (E, best Home result at 0.59%) | Unverified | Gameplay to confirm the backpack appears in the first 3 minutes; if not, replace the picture with the first real action |
| Icon with a huge Mythic head (brief 5) | NO | Change the icon to bacon plus a Rare or Epic cat plus net; Mythic only as a small sparkle |
| Title "Catch a Cat Cafe" | Yes | Keep |
| Detail page text | Check line by line | Remove any promise not in the first 3 minutes (draft 3b to be re-read) |
## Ads (cheap plays, no ranking): keep real. SECRET CAT! and 1 IN 67! ads are fine as "find" promises (our best volume ads held players 11+ minutes) but the picture must say "find", not "you get one now".
## Possible game change to make more of the promise true (Design call)
A visible glowing Mythic or Secret silhouette early (for example in the Alleyway or on the cannon card by 60 s), so a Mythic thumbnail would not be clickbait. Until Design ships that, no Mythic hero art on Home.
