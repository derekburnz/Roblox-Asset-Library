## Rules for the next Home picture or icon
- One big hero, one number or rarity word, three words or fewer, no title.
- Show the first minute of play and one cute thing a kid understands.
- Real in game things only, and every number must be a true game number.
- Nothing that matters in the bottom 15%. Words inside the 8% margins. Check it at 150 px.
- Eyes at about 40% height; never cover the hero's face.
- Use the real Roblox avatar, rendered well. Hand built or badly lit avatars were rejected.
- Keep five active and a spare for the 5 to 7 day rotation. Judge only pictures with 1,000 or more impressions, and wait 7 days between tests.
